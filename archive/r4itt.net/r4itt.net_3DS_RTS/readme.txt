R4iTT 3DS RTS is compatible with BL2CK R4 OS, which forks from the last official RTS firmware.

Download the BL2CK 1.31.0 kernel:
    https://archive.flashcarts.net/BL2CK/BL2CK_1.31.0.zip

R4iTTS 3DS RTS is also compatible with the R4iTT 'old' kernel:
    ../r4itt.net_3DS_NEW/old/r4itt.net_3DS_NEW_Kernel_2.37/

Instructions to update the Flashcart's firmware are under ./r4itt.net_3DS_RTS_FWUpdate_3DSV7.0.0/
