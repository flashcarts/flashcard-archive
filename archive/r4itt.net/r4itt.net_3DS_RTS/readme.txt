R4iTT 3DS RTS is compatible with BL2CK OS, which forks from the last official RTS firmware.

Download the BL2CK 1.31.0 kernel:
    https://archive.flashcarts.net/BL2CK/BL2CK_1.31.0.zip


Instructions to update the Flashcart's firmware are under ./r4itt.net_3DS_RTS_FWUpdate_3DSV7.0.0/

cheats:
    - BL2CK: __rpg/cheats/usrcheat.dat
    - r4itt.net_3DS_RTS_Kernel_5.01 (old) SYSTEM/cheats/usrcheat.dat
